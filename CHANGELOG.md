# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. Each release's evidence lives in [`evals/`](evals/).

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-28

Rebuilt around what the piece is for. Craft Essays now serves any writing that has to work on a reader, including decks, websites, talks and client presentations, and other skills can call it for a craft pass.

- Starts from purpose: the reader, what should change in them, what the writer wants and what must survive untouched.
- Offers the craft as tools chosen for that purpose, never as a style. A plain-version test keeps a crafted choice only when it does more for the reader than the plain version would.
- Names thirteen tools, including **Harvest**, which keeps the author's own asides and phrases in the work.
- Turns the essays' time-boxed bans into eight revision passes, ending with truth and completeness.
- Replaces the three references (structure, substance, expression) with four branches by medium: decks and talks, web and product, narrative, and sentences. [Origins](skills/craft-essays/references/origins.md) adds a page map to the source compilation.
- Keeps `??????` placeholders for drafts. Finished work narrows the claim and names the missing fact.
- Adds a craft pass for skills that hand over a draft, such as a deck or studio-voice skill: their brief, voice and markers govern, and the draft comes back in the same shape.
- Leaves the author's name out of the skill description, so the tools arrive without the style.
- Blind two-judge comparison on ten fresh briefs: [evals/v0.2.0](evals/v0.2.0/README.md). Design notes: [docs/v0.2-design.md](docs/v0.2-design.md).
