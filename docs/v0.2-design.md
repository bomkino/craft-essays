# Craft Essays v0.2 — design note

Written 28 September 2026 for maintainers. The [research foundation](foundation.md) remains the v0.1 source study; this note records what v0.2 changes and why.

## What was wrong with v0.1

The v0.1 instructions were careful, but they did not change the model's writing much. Across the [initial study](../evals/README.md), v0.1.0 beat unassisted writing in three of eight briefs, tied four and lost one. In the [longer follow-up](../evals/v0.1.1/README.md), v0.1.1 beat unassisted writing twice and lost twice to v0.1.0.

Reading the skill against the source and against writing-for-agents practice suggests why:

- **No leading words.** The source teaches through compact named tools: unpack, horses, received text, the buried gun, thought verbs, big voice and little voice, head and heart, the thumbnail. v0.1 translated every one into general advice ("examine the shortcut", "develop rather than restate"). General advice restates what a capable model already does by default, so it adds little.
- **Hedging everywhere.** Most instructions carried their exception in the same sentence. Preserving the source's tensions is right; stating every tension at full strength inside every move left no default behaviour for the agent to adopt.
- **A split that never disclosed anything.** Structure, substance and expression are dimensions of every substantial piece, so a real task loaded all three references. The eval logs confirm that the skill-assisted writer read all three.
- **No media.** Decks, websites and presentations of work, the forms studios write most, had no guidance.

The same source had also been adapted separately inside pitch.dog's writing and deck skills. v0.2 is meant to be the single home for the craft, so those skills can point to it instead of carrying their own copies.

## What v0.2 does

- **Purpose first.** The first step names the reader and what should change in them, what the writer wants, and what must survive. Every tool is chosen for that purpose.
- **Tools, not a style.** The source's tools are powerful and its author's voice is distinctive; an agent primed with his name drifts toward imitation. The always-loaded description therefore names the craft, not the author, and the skill states that the finished piece sounds like its author and its occasion. Author-signature devices (deliberately wrong phrasing, refrains, action-first openings as a default) appear only as options inside the branches where they belong.
- **The plain-version test.** Each crafted choice is compared with its plain version, and the version that does more for this reader wins. It keeps craft from becoming mannerism without licensing blandness.
- **Moves as leading words.** Twelve named moves sit in `SKILL.md`, most under the name the essays use. Each move states its target behaviour and its limit in positive terms.
- **Bans become revision passes.** The essays teach through time-boxed bans (six months without clichés, a month without pronouns) that the author himself treats as drills. v0.2 turns each ban into a pass: find every instance, then decide each one. The passes give the agent an exhaustive, checkable completion criterion instead of a word ban.
- **Thumbnail for practical writing.** The source's own overture form supplies the answer-first rule, so practical writing does not need an exception to the craft: when the reader must act, thumbnail; when the reader must feel or believe, let them arrive first.
- **`??????` placeholders.** The source's own drafting habit replaces both clichés and invented facts, and it makes gaps visible to the author.
- **Branches by medium.** References now split by what the task is: decks and talks, web and product, narrative, and sentence-level work. A task loads the branch it needs.
- **A craft-pass operation.** Other skills can hand over a draft; the calling skill's brief, voice and markers govern, and the draft comes back in the same shape.

## What stays

The v0.1 boundaries survive: no invented facts, figures, quotations, feelings or biography; the author's voice and useful roughness are protected; direct statements stay where the reader needs them; source exercises are not rules for finished work; the essays' scientific claims are not evidence. The foundation's worked cases remain the maintainer record of hard choices; they are no longer loaded at runtime.
