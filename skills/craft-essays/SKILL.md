# Craft Essays

Make the piece more perceptive, developed and effective in its own voice. The right result may be spare, exuberant, plain, lyrical, funny or difficult. Choose for the occasion.

## Find the work

Read the request and available material. Establish the intended reader, occasion and operation: draft, revise, critique or a bounded edit. Preserve the author's meaning, supplied facts, commitments, exact terms and requested constraints. Attend to what is already alive in the writing—its observations, opinions, rhythms, roughness and relationship with the reader.

Use what the context establishes. If no writing or brief is available, ask what the user wants to write or revise. Ask for further material only when its absence would materially change the result.

## Choose the intervention

For a narrow edit, make that edit. For substantial writing, identify the most consequential opportunity before polishing: missing material, an undeveloped thought, weak order, generic expression, monotonous rhythm or a lost voice. Ground the diagnosis in an actual passage or need. A new draft needs something worth saying and a reason for this reader to encounter it.

Read the reference that helps make the present decision:

- **Focus, order, development or endings:** [structure](references/structure.md). Choose disclosure, build an idea, manage inference and fulfil the opening's promise.
- **Thin claims, generic detail, unearned authority or comparison:** [substance](references/substance.md). Find relevant particulars, distinguish evidence from interpretation, and make an analogy do useful work.
- **Voice, cadence, emotional expression, humour or monotony:** [expression](references/expression.md). Preserve individuality, compose for the ear, change texture, and make room for feeling or play.

Load additional references when another decision needs them. A simple spelling correction can proceed without any. Treat the techniques as options with conditions, not as quotas to satisfy.

## Compose and reconsider

Write or revise the requested work. Develop thought as well as language: sometimes the necessary change is a sharper distinction, another implication, a better example or a different structure. Let a substantial exploratory draft discover its subject before demanding a finished surface from every sentence.

Return to the diagnosis and brief. Examine whether the change actually helps and what it costs. An edit that gains pace may lose tenderness; one that gains vividness may invent a fact. Retain the gain only when it serves the piece. Restore valuable qualities lost in revision. A successful passage can remain unchanged.

In factual and personal writing, draw specifics from supplied or verified material; keep inference and uncertainty recognisable. Where invention is invited, invent within the brief. Preserve deliberate ambiguity when the work depends on it. Exact instructions, practical answers and consequential qualifications deserve enough explicitness to be usable.

Return the writing in the requested form. Give editorial reasoning when asked, or briefly when needed to explain a material choice or missing information. Keep the working process proportionate to the task.

## Origins

These are original general-writing adaptations of lessons in Chuck Palahniuk's craft essays. Use the writer's own voice. Consult [origins](references/origins.md) for attribution or questions about the source; reading the original collection is not a prerequisite for using this skill.
