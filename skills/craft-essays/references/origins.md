# Origins and limits

Craft Essays adapts the monthly writing-craft essays Chuck Palahniuk wrote for readers online between 2005 and 2008. The studied compilation is a 160-page PDF titled *36 Writing Craft Essays by Chuck Palahniuk.pdf* (SHA-256 `be6591cec7b7c41b5ec4e74ae59b72d6c7f15aac395a8ff99143a4bb380c8c3e`). Its contents list 31 entries, including the three annotated acts of *Fetch*; the filename is not a claim of complete coverage. Palahniuk later developed this teaching into the book *Consider This* (2020).

Palahniuk credits the writers he learned from, and several moves carry their names: horses, received text and on-the-body writing come from Tom Spanbauer's workshop; hiding the "I" comes from Peter Christopher. Two concepts, thought verbs and big voice / little voice, belong to essays the compilation refers to but does not include; this skill uses them as they appear within it.

## Limits

The wording, examples and applications to general writing are this project's own. The essays are neither reproduced nor distributed with the skill, and the skill works without them. The moves are editorial guidance, not guarantees of any reader's response. The essays' medical, psychological, neurological and statistical claims are not adopted as evidence. Maintainer research lives in the repository's `docs/` and `evals/` folders and is not needed during a writing task.
